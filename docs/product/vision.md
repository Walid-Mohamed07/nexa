# Product vision

NEXA Café is a digital café platform designed to connect the customer, table, order, staff, and business intelligence within a single experience. The initial Phase 1 scope focuses on a mobile-first customer journey driven by QR table access, a shared domain model, a documented OpenAPI contract, and a mock environment that enables frontend and backend teams to move in parallel.

The customer journey should be frictionless: a guest can scan a QR, browse the menu without creating an account, add items to cart, and complete an order while staying within the branch and table context. The system must support both guest and returning customer states without forcing registration too early.

This product is intentionally designed for multi-tenant growth and future personalization, while keeping the first release lean and consistent across web, backend, and desktop development.
